# 🍕 Pizza Max Business Analysis — Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

**Pizza Max Business Analysis** is an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze pizza sales performance, customer ordering patterns, product performance, and overall business profitability.

The dashboard transforms sales data into actionable business insights through interactive KPIs, charts, filters, and product-level analysis.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze Pizza Max's business performance and answer important questions such as:

* How much revenue is being generated?
* How many orders and pizzas are being sold?
* Which pizza categories perform best?
* Which pizza sizes generate the highest sales?
* Which pizzas generate the most revenue?
* Which pizzas receive the highest number of orders?
* What are the monthly sales trends?
* What are the total costs and profits?
* What is the overall gross profit margin?

* **Total Pizzas Sold**

* **Total Cost**
* **Total Profit**
* **Gross Profit Margin**

These KPIs provide a high-level overview of overall business performance.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Monthly Sales Analysis

The dashboard analyzes **Total Sales by Month** to identify:

* Monthly revenue trends
* High-performing months
* Low-performing months
* Changes in sales performance over time

### 2. Daily Orders Analysis

The **Daily Orders Trend** provides insight into order activity and helps identify patterns in customer ordering behavior.

### 3. Pizza Category Analysis

Sales are analyzed across different pizza categories to determine which categories contribute most to overall revenue.

### 4. Pizza Size Analysis

The dashboard analyzes sales according to pizza size to understand customer preferences and revenue contribution.

### 5. Category & Size Analysis

The combination of **Pizza Category and Pizza Size** provides a more detailed view of product performance.

### 6. Pizza-Level Sales Analysis

Sales are analyzed at the individual pizza level to identify high- and low-performing products.

---

## 🏆 Product Performance Analysis

The dashboard includes interactive analysis of:

### Top & Bottom Pizza by Revenue

Identifies pizzas generating the:

* Highest revenue
* Lowest revenue

### Top & Bottom Pizza by Orders

Identifies pizzas with:

* Highest number of orders
* Lowest number of orders

### Top & Bottom Pizza by Quantity

Identifies pizzas with:

* Highest quantity sold
* Lowest quantity sold

This helps management understand which products are driving business performance.

## 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard Features

The dashboard contains interactive filters/slicers for:

* **Month**
* **Pizza Category**
* **Pizza Size**
* **Pizza Name**

Users can interactively filter the dashboard to analyze specific segments.

A **Remove Filters** button is also included to quickly reset the dashboard.

## 🧮 DAX Measures

The project uses DAX measures to calculate important business KPIs, including:

* Total Orders
* Total Pizzas Sold
* Average Order
* Total Sales
* Total Cost
* Total Profit
* Gross Profit Margin

These measures enable dynamic calculations that respond to the selected dashboard filters.
